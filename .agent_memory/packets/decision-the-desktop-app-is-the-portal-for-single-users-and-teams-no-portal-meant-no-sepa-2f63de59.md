# The desktop app IS the portal — for single users AND teams; 'no portal' meant no separate web page

> 2026 07 31, Kushal, voice discussion, correcting a misreading of the 2026 07 29 decision: "When I said it won't have …

2026-07-31, Kushal, voice discussion, correcting a misreading of the 2026-07-29 decision: "When I said it won't have portal, I mean it won't have a web page, and it'll be a desktop app, and that will become the portal." Kage serves BOTH single users and teams — each person's desktop app is their portal into memory, including a team's shared memory. The "single-user" gloss in the prior packet was wrong and must not be inherited. Direction articulated in the same discussion (positioning still being defined, not final): agent sessions automatically deposit important decisions as packets; the compounding, code-grounded knowledge becomes the foundation the SDLC runs on — PRDs grounded in what the team already knows, decomposed into tasks, assigned to team members and to agent roles (plan / build / test), features built from tickets with memory injected, and verified via gates and receipts. Teams build and exchange memory as the foundation for their features.
